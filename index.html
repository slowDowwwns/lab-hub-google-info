Local desktop workspace

Lab Hub

Your work, mail and calendar in one local workspace.

Lab Hub is a desktop application for organizing projects, notes and daily work. Its optional Google connection brings mail and calendar information into the application.

Google mail and calendar

You choose which Google accounts and services to connect. With the permissions you grant, Lab Hub can read mail and calendars, create or update calendar events, and perform mail actions such as managing messages, drafts and sending mail. Available controls depend on your installed version and account permissions.

Sign-in takes place on Google's website. Lab Hub does not ask for or store your Google password. You can disconnect an account in Lab Hub or revoke its access in your Google Account.

This website provides application information and policies. It does not connect to your Google account or display your mail or calendar.

About this deployment

This Google integration is intended for the owner's personal use. A work account remains subject to its organization's access rules. This website does not grant Google access or replace those rules.

Read the privacy policy for details about Google data and local storage.
