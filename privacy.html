Privacy policy

Google mail and calendar integration in the local Lab Hub desktop application.

Data accessed

When you connect Google, Lab Hub receives authorization tokens and your verified account identity, including your email address and Google account identifier. Depending on the services and permissions you select, it can access calendar lists, event details, message headers, labels, previews and message content. Mail content is fetched when you open a message.

How data is used

Lab Hub uses this data to identify the connected account, display mail and events, synchronize information, and perform actions you request. Calendar permissions allow reading calendars and creating, updating or deleting events. Mail permissions allow reading and managing messages, drafts and sending mail. Write operations require the corresponding Google permission.

Storage and security

The integration runs in the desktop application. Google authorization tokens and the mail metadata cache are stored locally using the operating system's protected encryption service. The connection is unavailable if secure token storage is unavailable. Network requests to Google use HTTPS. Lab Hub does not store your Google password.

Information you choose to copy into notes, documents, exports or other local files is separate from the integration cache and follows the storage and backup settings for those files. Access to your computer and its backups remains your responsibility.

Sharing and optional services

Google data is used for the features described above, not sold or used for advertising. The public information website does not receive your Google tokens, mail or calendar data. Actions such as sending mail or inviting event guests transmit the data you choose through Google to the intended recipients.

Lab Hub also has optional AI and external-service features. If you include copied Google content in material you submit to such a feature, that content may be sent to the provider you configured. Review that provider's policy and your organization's rules before doing so. This policy does not promise that content you deliberately export or share stays on your computer.

Retention and removal

Disconnecting a Google account removes its saved connection and mail metadata cache from Lab Hub. You can also revoke Lab Hub's authorization in your Google Account. Disconnecting does not delete the original mail or calendar data held by Google. Separately saved notes, exports, local history and backups are not automatically erased; remove them separately if needed.

Google API Limited Use

Lab Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Lab Hub does not use the Google mail and calendar connection to train general-purpose AI models.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, forms or account sign-in. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visitor information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about this integration or policy, contact labhubsupport@gmail.com.
