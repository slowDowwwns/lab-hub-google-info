These terms describe the Google integration configured for the owner's personal use in Lab Hub. This information website does not distribute the application or create a public hosted mail service.

Account access

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to use. For work accounts, follow your organization's requirements. Lab Hub does not override Google or organization access controls.

Your actions

Review the selected account, recipients and event details before performing changes. Sending messages or editing events affects the corresponding Google account and may notify other people. Features depend on the permissions you grant, your installed version and Google's availability.

Control and privacy

You can stop using the integration, disconnect an account in Lab Hub, or revoke access in Google. See the privacy policy for data handling and removal details.
